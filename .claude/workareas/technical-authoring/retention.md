# Authoring: What Is Retained

This record states, for each piece of existing material, the design file that uses it and the purpose, or the reason the material is not retained. It follows `source-map.md`, which holds the line references. The design files are those in `design.md`: `SKILL.md`, `writing.md`, `line-editing.md`, `plan-template.md` and `checks.md`.

## language.md

- "Voice and tone" is used in `writing.md`, tone of voice, for the purpose, the register and the relation to the reader. The rule against addressing the reader as `you` is also a row in `checks.md`.
- "Register" is used in `line-editing.md` for the test of framing that carries a judgement and its repair, and the worked renewal example becomes a line-editing pair.
- "Sentences and paragraphs" is used in `writing.md`, paragraphs and sentences, for the drafting rules. The split at the seam and the moved condition are used in `line-editing.md` as repairs. The calibration narrative is not retained, because it records how a rule was found, not the rule.
- "Actors and voice" is used in `writing.md`, sentences, for the actor as subject and the passive kept where the actor is unknown.
- "References and pronouns" is used in `writing.md`, sentences, for repeating the noun, and in `line-editing.md` for the unclear-reference test and the question raised when the text does not settle the antecedent. The sentence ending on `it` is a pattern in `checks.md`.
- "Verbs and noun stacks" is used in `writing.md`, sentences, for the actor plus verb and unpacked noun stacks. The slang verbs are rows in `checks.md`.
- "Write it forward" is used in `writing.md`, sentences, for its four drafting rules.
- "Reader-pays compression" points to the compression patterns, which are used in `line-editing.md` as recognition tests.
- "Just Enough" is used in `writing.md`, paragraphs, for the definition of a finished paragraph, and in `line-editing.md` for the burden of proof on additions, whole-unit removal, no condensation and tangents. The clarify and distil intents are not retained, because one rule now governs growth: every addition gives its reason.
- The default removals in "Just Enough" are used in `line-editing.md` as the noise and framing tests. Filler words are rows in `checks.md`. Undecided mechanisms are not retained in authoring, because what the document claims is the plan's concern, and they move to document-planning.
- "Paragraph Restatement Test" is used in `SKILL.md` as the restatement test.
- "The whole document" is used in `SKILL.md` as the closing-pass checklist.
- "Evidence and attribution" is used in `writing.md`, tone of voice, for the attitude to claims: attribution with a document and date, absolutes with their limit, and dates in place of timebound words. "Never invent a fact" is used in `SKILL.md` as the rule that a gap becomes a question.
- "Terms and abbreviations" is used in `writing.md`, sentences, for first use, internal labels and one name for one thing. British spelling is rows in `checks.md`.
- "Words to avoid or examine" is used in `checks.md` as the word table, every row a Change rule with its legitimate uses written in as allowed forms. The tier text and the tiering procedure are not retained, because decision 12 abolishes tiers. Each word "listed and not checked" becomes a row, a writing rule, or is dropped when `checks.md` is written.
- "Punctuation and formatting" is used in `checks.md` for the dashes, semicolons, quotes, spaced hyphens and `N/A`, and in `writing.md` for the negative contraction, ranges and the other rules a script cannot test.
- "Code and identifiers" is used in `writing.md`, forms, for code font, fences and naming a schema definition with its property. The masking statement is a behaviour of `hedy prose check`.
- "Headings, lists and tables" is used in `writing.md`, forms, for structure in proportion, concrete headings, bold lead-ins and the opener that states the point. FAQ voice is stated in `writing.md` as a rule and tested in `checks.md` as patterns. The inventory opener is not retained, because decision 14 deletes the rule.
- "Technical usage" is used in `checks.md` as the term list's prefer-and-avoid rows, and in `writing.md`, sentences, for the requirement words.
- "Capitalisation, numbers, dates and inclusion" is used in `writing.md`, sentences. Heading title case is a Fix rule in `checks.md`.
- "References and examples" is used in `writing.md`, forms, for links that resolve, no gitignored citations, and the two-link limit.
- The opening paragraph and the "Gate" notes are not retained, because they describe the old gate.

## authoring-voice.md

- The paths list is used in the house-standard rule, to set the scope of the write hook.
- The drafting digest is not retained, because `writing.md` is the one home for the standard.
- The two exemplars are used in `writing.md` as sentence pairs.
- Reading a neighbouring page before drafting is not retained, because authoring writes only from the plan and the writing guide.

## The Editorial Skill's References

- `document-types.md` is used in `writing.md`, document types and forms. Its fixed-format artefacts are used in `SKILL.md` as the artefact forms.
- `voice-examples.md` is used in `writing.md` as before-and-after pairs.
- `clarify-examples.md` is used in `line-editing.md` as before-and-after pairs.
- `explain-and-rewrite.md` is used in `SKILL.md` for the unpacking procedure, steps 1 to 3 and 6. The claim-comparison script and the fresh-context reader in steps 4 and 5 are not retained, because the design has no script for claims and no model cold reader.
- The compression patterns in `review-brief.md` are used in `line-editing.md` as recognition tests. Its rules for every reviewer are used in `line-editing.md`: change only where the reader gains, and code and quotations are exempt. The finding schema, reviewer roles and review procedure are not retained, because the design has no review findings.
- The paragraph questions in `layer-cards.md` are used in `line-editing.md`. The page questions are used in `SKILL.md`'s closing pass. The set questions are not retained in authoring, because repetition across pages and reading order belong to document-planning.
- The editorial `SKILL.md`'s reader frame is used in `plan-template.md` for the reader and purpose. Its hand-over is used in `SKILL.md`'s hand-over report. Its review, triage and cost steps are not retained.

## Other Writing Practice Files

- `ubiquitous-language.md` is used in `checks.md`'s term list, for the registered names that pass unexpanded.
- `commits.md`, `pull-requests.md` and the pr and jira skills are used in `SKILL.md` for the artefact forms.

## The Grading Rubric

- H-01, H-03, H-04, H-07, H-13, H-18, H-19, H-21, H-23, H-25, H-26, H-27, H-28 and H-31 are used in `line-editing.md` as tests and repairs.
- H-05 is used in `writing.md`, paragraphs: a rule with a home elsewhere is not restated.
- H-32 is retained as the FAQ-voice pattern in `checks.md`.
- H-02 is not retained in authoring, because undecided mechanisms are the plan's concern.
- H-06, H-08, H-14 and H-15 are not retained, because they judge contradiction, truth or completeness, which belong to fact checking and planning.
- The moves, verdicts, calibration and scoring are not retained, because the design has no grading.

## Preferences Held in Memory

- Natural prose over compressed idiom, plain framing of a standard pattern, writing the worst case, and showing the release design are used in `writing.md`, tone of voice.
- No working codes in reader-facing prose is used in `writing.md`, sentences.
- The two-link limit and numbered diagram notes are used in `writing.md`, forms.
- One agreed sentence per section is used in `plan-template.md` as each section's point.
- An ADR states requirements, not design, is used in `writing.md`, document types.
- A ticket states why, what and constraints is used in `SKILL.md`'s ticket form.
- A map by intent for a large output and the word count with every edit are used in `SKILL.md`'s hand-over report.
- The author's supplied wording and cuts are used in `line-editing.md` as the author's rights. The author's headings keep their wording, and the checks still apply: hedy fixes title case automatically, and a heading in FAQ voice is a Change hit that authoring rewords.
- Rules stay general is used in `design.md` as the upkeep rule for `writing.md` and `checks.md`.
- Hard wrapping for commit messages only is used in `writing.md`, forms.
- A rule plus its edge-case handling, and completion of an incomplete promise, are not retained, because Ben judged them too narrow to be useful.
- The blind pick of up to three versions is not retained in the skill, because the blind pick is how Ben judges the proof, not a step authoring runs.

## The INS Auth Profile

- The reader frame is used in `plan-template.md` as the worked example of a reader.
- The preferred names are used in `checks.md`'s term list.
- The run notes are not retained in the MVP, because data for each document set waits until two sets need different names.

## Not Retained at All

- The editorial-triage, editorial-help, editorial-metrics and design-consistency skills and tools. They are process machinery for review findings, grading, cost and contradiction, and the design has none of those processes.
- Every script in the estate. hedy holds all code, and the prose gate's behaviour is ported from its test cases, not its code.
